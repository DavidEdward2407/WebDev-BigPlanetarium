About the Big Planetarium

The Big Planetarium is a Bristol-based educational space dedicated to exploring the wonders of the universe. Our goal is to inspire curiosity and understanding of space science through engaging and accessible experiences.

Our Mission

We aim to make astronomy and space science accessible to everyone, regardless of age, background, or ability. By combining clear visual design with informative content, we help visitors explore complex ideas in an approachable way.
