What Is the Milky Way?

The Milky Way is a vast spiral galaxy that contains our solar system, along with billions of other stars, planets, and cosmic objects.

It stretches over 100,000 light-years across and is constantly moving through space as part of an even larger universe.

Why Is the Milky Way Important?

Studying the Milky Way helps scientists understand how galaxies form, how stars are born, and how our own planet came to exist.

By exploring our galaxy, we learn more about our place in the universe and the incredible scale of space around us.
